# [[Neom]] 

#is_/same_as :: [[WD~Neom,42310572]]

## #has_/text_of_/abstract 

> **Neom** (Arabic: نيوم, romanized: Niyōm; Hejazi Arabic pronunciation: [nɪˈjo̞ːm]; stylized as NEOM) 
> is a megaproject and planned city in Tabuk Province in Saudi Arabia. 
> Crown Prince Mohammad bin Salman, the project's main supporter and chairman, launched it in 2017. 
>
> The project was part of Saudi Vision 2030, with the intent being 
> to diversify the country’s oil-dependent economy 
> by creating a sustainable, carbon-neutral smart city 
> powered by renewable energy and advanced technology. 
> 
> At its peak, Neom was the world's largest construction project. 
> The site is at the northern tip of the Red Sea, 
> east of Egypt across the Gulf of Aqaba and close to Jordan. 
> Neom's total planned area was 26,500 km2 (10,200 sq mi). 
> Planners envisioned multiple regions, including 
> - a floating industrial complex, 
> - a global trade hub, and 
> - tourist resorts.
>
> However, Neom faced heavy criticism for its unrealistic plans. 
> Costs were originally estimated at $1.6 trillion, growing to $8.8 trillion by 2025—
> more than 25 times the annual Saudi governmental budget. 
>
> By July 2022, only two buildings had been constructed, 
> and most of the project area remained bare desert. 
> By 2024, there were reports that Neom's 
> size and scale would be sharply reduced from original plans. 
> An internal audit of Neom found extensive financial problems, 
> including "evidence of deliberate manipulation" by Neom management. 
> By 2025, new contracts for Neom ceased and there was 
> no mention of Neom in Saudi Arabia's pre-budget statement for 2026. 
> In March 2026, large contracts for extensive work were cancelled. 
>
> Neom has also been criticized for environmental destruction and human rights violations. 
> There are reports of high rates of death among workers on-site. 
> An estimated 20,000 people are subject to forced displacement, 
> and multiple villages have been razed. 
> 
> Members of the local Howeitat tribe protested against their forced expulsion. 
> Howeitat members have been killed resisting evictions, 
> including activist Abdul Rahim al-Huwaiti, 
> who was killed by Saudi security forces under disputed circumstances. 
> Three other members of the tribe were sentenced to death.
>
